UI/UX EXPERIMENT – HEURISTIC EVALUATION
AIM
To conduct a heuristic evaluation and comparison of two popular food delivery applications, Swiggy and Zomato, based on usability principles such as visibility of system status, consistency, user control, error prevention, recognition, flexibility, visual hierarchy, navigation, and feedback, and to identify improvements that can be applied to a proposed food delivery application.

Consistency and standards

Accessibility
Compare Swiggy and Zomato based on their navigation, search, categories, restaurant cards, offers, filters, cart, checkout, payment, and order tracking.

CASE STUDY
Swiggy
Swiggy is a food delivery platform that allows users to search and discover restaurants, browse menus, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries. Its interface also provides features such as offers, restaurant discovery and other services.

Zomato
Zomato is a food discovery and delivery platform that allows users to search for restaurants, cuisines and dishes, explore restaurant information, place orders and track deliveries. It also provides features such as collections, offers and scheduled ordering.

HEURISTIC FINDINGS
SWIGGY – Strengths
Easy restaurant and food discovery.
Search is easily accessible.
Restaurant information is clearly presented.
Menu and cart flow is easy to understand.
Order status and delivery tracking are clearly communicated.
Offers and categories help users discover food.
Multiple services are available within the platform.
SWIGGY – AREAS FOR IMPROVEMENT
Multiple categories and promotional sections can make the interface information-heavy.
Too many offers may compete for the user's attention.
Important actions should have stronger visual priority.
The interface can be simplified by reducing unnecessary visual elements.
ZOMATO – Strengths
Strong restaurant and food discovery.
Search supports restaurants, cuisines and dishes.
Restaurant information, ratings and reviews help users make decisions.
Offers and collections improve food discovery.
Order scheduling provides additional flexibility.
Search and discovery are important parts of the overall Zomato experience.
ZOMATO – AREAS FOR IMPROVEMENT
Large amounts of content can sometimes make the interface feel crowded.
Multiple discovery sections may require better visual hierarchy.
Too many choices can increase decision-making time.
Important actions such as ordering and checkout should remain visually prominent.
PROPOSED IMPROVEMENTS
Based on the heuristic evaluation of Swiggy and Zomato, the following improvements can be applied to a proposed food delivery application:

Simplified Navigation

Use a simple bottom navigation bar.
Keep important sections easily accessible.
Prominent Search

Place the search bar in a highly visible position.
Allow users to search restaurants, dishes and cuisines.
Quick Categories

Provide categories such as Biryani, Pizza, Burger, South Indian, Chinese and Desserts.
Improved Restaurant Cards

Display restaurant name, rating, cuisine, delivery time and price clearly.
Better Food Cards

Use clear food images.
Display food name, price, rating and description.
Provide a visible Add button.
Improved Filters

Provide filters such as rating, price, delivery time, vegetarian and offers.
Visible Cart

Keep the cart easily accessible.
Show the number of selected items.
Simplified Checkout

Reduce unnecessary checkout steps.
Clearly display address, items, delivery charges and total amount.
Clear Payment Interface

Provide UPI, card, wallet and cash/payment options where applicable.
Clearly show the final payable amount.
Better Order Tracking

Display order stages such as:
Order Confirmed
Food Preparing
Picked Up
Out for Delivery
Delivered
Improved Visual Hierarchy
Give more importance to important actions.
Avoid excessive promotional content.
Accessibility
Use readable font sizes.
Maintain sufficient colour contrast.
Use clear icons and labels.
